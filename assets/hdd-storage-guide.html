PlayStation's rules for USB extended storage: SuperSpeed USB 5 Gbps or faster, 250 GB to 8 TB, plugged straight into the console (no USB hub), and one extended-storage drive at a time. PlayStation: USB extended storage on PS5.

When you actually need an SSD

Want lots of PS5 games ready to play, with no copying back and forth? Get an internal M.2 SSD with a heatsink. It goes inside the console: more living room.
