Optional PS5 storage upgrades

Check your free space first. Open Settings → Storage on your PS5. If you already have enough room, you do not need an SSD. Removing games you no longer need is a free way to make space; follow PlayStation’s guidance and keep your saved data safe.

Installing an M.2 solid-state drive (SSD)

Check your model before removing the cover. The original PS5 (CFI-1000) uses a large slide-off panel. On PS5 Pro (CFI-7000), lift the smaller triangle-marked cover to release its clips. The core steps inside the SSD bay are similar. If you have a PS5 Slim or Pro, choose CFI-2000/7000 in Sony’s official installation guide. Pro owners can also use the CFI-7000-series manual’s cover diagrams.

Original PS5 (CFI-1000) photo walkthrough. Use iFixit’s step-by-step photos of a real console alongside the original-model section of Sony’s guide. The photos show the original PS5, not the Slim or Pro.

  1. Get set up: Fully shut down, unplug the cables, let the console cool, and discharge static before opening it.
  2. Remove the outer panel: Look at photo steps 7–8 to see which corner lifts and how the panel slides off.
  3. Open the SSD bay and move the spacer: Photo steps 9–12 show the metal bay cover and where the spacer goes for your drive length.
  4. Install the drive: Photo steps 13–15 show the angle and screw placement, but the drive pictured is not suitable for PS5. Use a compatible drive with a cooling structure, and follow Sony’s instructions and illustrations to seat and secure it.
  5. Close up before powering on: Reattach the bay cover and outer panel. Reconnect power, then follow the format prompt if it appears. Formatting erases data on the added drive.

Specifications-based options, not hands-on reviews. Manufacturers’ peak read ratings are not PS5 load-time tests or proof of longer drive life. Usable capacity is lower than the advertised capacity. Check the exact model, capacity, heatsink, seller and current price before buying. Follow Sony’s installation guide: first-time formatting erases data already on the added SSD.
