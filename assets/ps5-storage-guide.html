Optional PS5 storage upgrades

Check your free space first. Open Settings → Storage on your PS5. If you already have enough room, you do not need an SSD. Removing games you no longer need is a free way to make space; follow PlayStation’s guidance and keep your saved data safe.

Our baseline: 2TB for ongoing PS5 use. Consider more only if your growing library genuinely needs it. Wait if you already have enough space or expect to change consoles soon. These are editorial Good / Great / Best options, not a current-price ranking or a promise of better gameplay.
