Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Who this covers

This policy covers the XFD Studios website (xfdstudios.github.io) and XFD Studio, a private tool XFD Studios uses to read analytics for its own social media accounts. "We" means XFD Studios (XenoFinalDawn).

The XFD Studio app and Google user data

The dashboard connects only to the XFD Studios YouTube channel, signed in by the channel's owner. It does not collect data from website visitors or from anyone else's Google account.

What we access

  • The channel's ID and name (YouTube Data API, youtube.readonly).
  • For each video uploaded to the channel: its video ID, title, thumbnail link, publish date, length, and its public view, like and comment counts (YouTube Data API, youtube.readonly). Thumbnail images are shown from YouTube's own links and are not downloaded.
  • Daily channel totals for the last 28 days: views, watch time, average view duration, likes, comments, shares and subscribers gained or lost (YouTube Analytics API, yt-analytics.readonly).
  • The same kinds of numbers for each video over the last 28 days, plus the average percentage of the video watched (YouTube Analytics API, yt-analytics.readonly).

The YouTube connection is read-only: it cannot upload, edit, delete or post anything on YouTube. (The dashboard does save its own notes and reports on the creator's computer, but it never writes to YouTube.)

How we use it

Only to show XFD Studios charts and reports about its own channel and to plan future videos. We do not sell this data, use it for advertising, or use it to build profiles of people.

Where it is stored

Each sync saves a dated snapshot in a database on the XFD Studios creator's own computer: the channel name and that day's 28-day channel report, and each video's details and numbers as they were at that time. When a video's numbers change, a sync adds a new snapshot and leaves older ones as they were, so the dashboard can show how each video grew over time; when nothing changed, it only refreshes the newest snapshot instead of saving a duplicate. When XFD Studios links one of its YouTube videos to one of its own projects (for example a Short made from a longer video), only the video ID is saved with that link; the title and numbers are read from the snapshots above. The Google sign-in token is saved in a private file on that same computer. The dashboard is configured to listen only on that computer, not on the network.

AI assistants

XFD Studios uses AI assistants (such as Anthropic's Claude, OpenAI's ChatGPT/Codex and Google's Antigravity/Gemini) to help read its own reports. This only happens when the channel owner asks an assistant to review a report. Only the report numbers and video titles needed for that question are shared, never the Google sign-in token, passwords or other credentials. Each AI provider handles what it receives under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not share YouTube data with anyone else, sell it, or use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

While the dashboard is running, it applies these rules automatically after every sync (about every 6 hours), including to its backup copies:

  • YouTube numbers (channel totals and each video's views, likes, comments, watch time and the other figures above) are kept for as long as XFD Studios needs them to compare how videos do over time, but only while authorization is confirmed by a successful sync at least every 30 days.
  • If no sync has confirmed authorization for 30 days, the YouTube API data stored in the dashboard's database and its backups is permanently deleted. The dashboard also asks Google to revoke the sign-in token, and deletes the token once Google confirms or reports it is no longer valid. If Google cannot be reached, the token is kept only so the request can be retried.
  • The channel name and ID, and each video's title, thumbnail link, publish date, length and link are removed from snapshots older than 30 days. The newest snapshot is refreshed by every sync, so current titles stay up to date.
  • If any part of a deletion fails, it is flagged in the dashboard and retried on every sync until it is complete.

If the dashboard has been turned off, these rules run within a few minutes of it starting again.

Removing access and deleting data

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account Third-party connections page. After that, the dashboard can no longer read anything new. At any time, the channel owner can use the dashboard's "Delete YouTube data" button to permanently delete the YouTube API data stored in the dashboard's database and its backups, and have the sign-in token revoked and deleted in the same way. Requests can also be sent through the contact page; they are handled as soon as possible and within 7 days.

The button only covers what the dashboard itself stores. It does not erase report numbers that were already shared with an AI assistant, or copied into notes or documents outside the dashboard. It also does not affect anything stored on YouTube itself; to delete data on YouTube, use YouTube or YouTube Studio.

Google and YouTube policies

XFD Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. The dashboard uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

This website

  • We do not run analytics or ad trackers on this website.
  • Messages sent through the contact form (name, email, topic, message) are delivered to us by Formspree and used only to reply.
  • Some pages show videos or posts from YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, Instagram or Threads, and the shop uses Fourthwall and Redbubble. Those services may set their own cookies under their own privacy policies.
  • The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions can be sent through the contact page.
