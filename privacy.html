Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Who this covers

This policy covers the XFD website (xfdstudios.github.io). "We" means XFD (XenoFinalDawn).

Analytics, forms, and hosting

  • Google Analytics is optional. We load its tag and allow its analytics cookies only after you select Accept analytics. Declining does not limit access to the website. Use the Analytics choices button in the footer to change your mind or withdraw consent; withdrawing stops collection and removes this site's Google Analytics cookies from your browser.
  • If you accept, Google processes page visits and outgoing link clicks, along with technical information such as browser/device information, approximate location and analytics cookie identifiers, to help us understand website use. We do not send contact-form contents, names, email addresses or account IDs to Analytics. We strip page query strings and fragments and do not send the referring page URL. Advertising personalization and Google Signals are disabled in our tag.
  • Your accept or decline choice is saved in this browser for 180 days using local storage. Analytics cookies are configured to expire after 180 days. Google handles analytics data under its Privacy Policy; learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services.
  • Messages sent through the contact form (name, email, topic, message) are delivered to us by Formspree and used only to reply.
  • Some pages show videos or posts from YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, Instagram or Threads, and the shop uses Fourthwall and Redbubble. Those services may set their own cookies under their own privacy policies.
  • The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security.

Affiliate links and disclosures

Some guides and resource pages on this website contain affiliate links. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

  • When you click an Amazon affiliate link on this site and make a qualifying purchase, XFD may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you.
  • Affiliate links are clearly disclosed near the top of the page before the first link and marked with rel="noopener sponsored".
  • Recommendations are based on public product specifications and topic fit, not paid placements. Amazon handles any clicks, cookies, or purchases made on Amazon.com under Amazon's own Privacy Notice.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions can be sent through the contact page.
